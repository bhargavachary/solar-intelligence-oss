📥Banking: Surplus solar units exported to TPSODL grid are credited to your account — like depositing electricity into a bank.
📤Settlement: Night-time consumption first draws from banked units. You pay only for the net difference (Import − Export).
🔄Carry Forward: Surplus units carry over month-to-month. Annual settlement (March 31) pays remaining surplus at ₹3.28/unit (APPC rate FY 24-25).

💡 Fixed charges are unavoidable: ₹20/kW/month (₹40 for 2kW) + ₹60 smart meter rent = ₹100 minimum bill even at net-zero. Electricity Duty 4% applies on total.
🚫 Common misconception: You cannot simply export all solar units and get paid. TPSODL requires you to consume at least 90% of your generation before any surplus qualifies for payout. Residual surplus (if any) is paid at just ~₹2.50/unit (APPC rate), not the retail tariff of ₹4-6/unit. Solar is for self-consumption savings, not for selling electricity.
